# Notes

Activity
Ask:

“How much federal money came into your state last year?”
“Which companies near you receive federal contracts?”

Most people don’t know. That’s the hook.

Take note of:

- Award spending by state
- Contract spending by agency
- Spending by NAICS

Reports:

**1. Federal Spending by State**

Business Question: Which states receive the most federal contract money?

Insights to Teach

- Is spending concentrated?
- Does spending correlate with population?
- Which regions depend heavily on federal contracts?
- Does your state rely on defense contracts?
- Real Life Impact Discussion

If your state gets high defense spending:
→ Local job opportunities in manufacturing & engineering.

If healthcare spending increases:
→ Hospitals expanding hiring.

If infrastructure spending rises:
→ Construction growth.

This connects data to job strategy.

**2 Agency Spending Trends**

Business Question: Which federal agencies spend the most and how is it changing?

Group by:

- Department of Defense
- Department of Health
- Department of Transportation

Insight Angles

- Which agencies are expanding?
- Which are shrinking?
- Are there budget shifts?

Real-Life Relevance

If Transportation spending rises:

→ Road projects

→ Contract opportunities

→ Regional employment increase

**3 Industry (NAICS) Analysis**

Business Question: Which industries receive the most federal money?

Interpretation

- Is tech growing?
- Is construction rising?
- Is cybersecurity increasing?

This tells:

Students which IT field is safest.

Business owners where opportunity exists.
